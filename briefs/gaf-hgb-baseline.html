The metric

Two qualifying paths

A single strict "1 anchor + 2 attachments" test undercounts our strongest builders — a cart of an All-In-One trainer plus a treadmill is two centrepieces but reads as only one anchor and one attachment. A second path fixes that.

Path A · Anchor + build-out

One centrepiece, kitted out

One anchor product, plus at least two attachment items, with a total cart value over the threshold. The everyday home-gym build.

1 anchor  +  2 attachments  +  cart > $3,376
1,441 of the 1,580 qualifying sales (91%)
Path B · Multi-anchor

Two centrepieces in one cart

Two or more anchor products, with a total cart value over the threshold — regardless of attachment count. Two centrepieces is self-evidently a gym build.

2+ anchors  +  cart > $3,376
139 of the 1,580 qualifying sales (9%)

The $3,376 gate is the average Home Gym Builder cart value from our archetype economics. The lever that makes the metric decisive is the anchor list, not the gate — anchors are project-defining training centrepieces only.

Is the metric decisive?

It rejects 58% of big carts as "not a build"

Method & assumptions

How the number was built

So the team can trust, challenge and reproduce it.

Scope. All Gym & Fitness (NetSuite subsidiary "Gym & Fitness Inc") sales orders dated 1 Jul 2025 – 30 Jun 2026, all order statuses that represent a real sale (billed, closed, pending & partial fulfilment; no cancellations present). 13,812 orders. Read-only throughout.
Value gate. Order total over $3,376, inclusive of GST and freight (the cart value the customer is charged). 3,759 orders clear it.
Composition. For each cart, the priced order lines were taken (kit parents counted once, their exploded component and box lines collapsed to the parent, tax/discount/shipping lines dropped), each distinct product mapped to its sub-category, then tagged anchor / attachment / neither and the two-path rule applied.
Attachments are counted by distinct SKU — two different attachment products, per Adam's "any 2 other SKUs" rule. A single line bought in quantity 2 is one attachment, not two.
Coverage. 100% of the 14,005 priced lines were classified — the ~75% that matched the categorisation sheet directly, plus 68 bundle/variant SKUs (3,518 lines) resolved by hand from their NetSuite descriptions. Unresolved lines were treated as "neither" (the conservative direction — it cannot manufacture a false builder).
Known judgement calls, flagged not hidden. "VF-RTE15" resolved to Versa-Fit flooring tiles (an attachment), not a treadmill. The "50lb weight-stack upgrade" and cable/landmine attachments were treated as neither (all-in-one accessories), which slightly tightens Path A. Re-running once the sheet absorbs these SKUs will firm the number.
