The metric

Two qualifying paths

A single strict "1 anchor + 2 attachments" test undercounts our strongest builders — a cart of an All-In-One trainer plus a treadmill is two centrepieces but reads as only one anchor and one attachment. A second path fixes that.

Path A · Anchor + build-out

One centrepiece, kitted out

One anchor product, plus at least two attachment items, with a total cart value over the threshold. The everyday home-gym build.

1 anchor  +  2 attachments  +  cart > $3,376
Path B · Multi-anchor

Two centrepieces in one cart

Two or more anchor products, with a total cart value over the threshold — regardless of attachment count. Two centrepieces is self-evidently a gym build.

2+ anchors  +  cart > $3,376

The $3,376 gate is the average Home Gym Builder cart value from our archetype economics. The lever that makes the metric decisive is the anchor list, not the gate — anchors are project-defining training centrepieces only.

The anchor set · 14 sub-categories

Project-defining centrepieces only

Every anchor is a big-ticket training centrepiece that implies a build-out. Saunas, ice baths and pin/plate-loaded machines were deliberately dropped from the anchor slot — a $6k sauna clears the gate on its own without a gym being built, and it is Revel's wellness category, not gym demand. They still count as attachments once a training anchor is present.

The final step

Baseline count & monthly trend — in build

What remains: the cart-composition filter

Turning the 1,896 qualifying-value carts into the true Home Gym Builder count needs one thing the item-master and order-header exports don't hold — order-line detail (which sub-categories sit inside each cart). That is a read-only line-level pull from the NetSuite saved search, joined to the sub-category map, then the two-path rule applied per order.

  1. Pull sales-order lines for the trailing 12 months where order total > $3,376 (read only).
  2. Tag each line's sub-category → anchor / attachment / neither.
  3. Flag each order: Path A (1 anchor + 2 attachments) or Path B (2+ anchors).
  4. Count qualifying orders → the baseline. Split by month → the trend. Split by customer type → channel mix.

This page updates with the headline baseline number, the monthly trend and the channel split the moment that line-level export lands.
