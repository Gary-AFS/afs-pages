Install & use

What this is

A Chrome extension for the AFS team that records your screen and webcam and microphone into one video. A draggable webcam bubble and recording toolbar follow you across tabs so you always have control. Recordings are saved locally to your machine. To share a recording, drag the file into Google Drive and copy the link.

FeatureDetail
CameraWebcam composited into the video as a draggable bubble
AudioMicrophone captured alongside the screen
Output.webm file saved directly to your Downloads folder
SharingDrag the file into Google Drive for a shareable link
CostZero. No servers, no accounts, no subscriptions

Install on Chrome / Chromebook

  1. Download the zip above and unzip it. You should get a folder with manifest.json, a src/ folder, and an icons/ folder directly inside (no nested subfolder). On ChromeOS, open Files, find the zip, right-click and choose Extract.
  2. Open Chrome and go to chrome://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode using the toggle in the top-right corner.
  4. Click Load unpacked (top-left) and select the unzipped folder - the one containing manifest.json. On ChromeOS, you can also drag the folder directly onto the extensions page.
  5. The AFS Screen Recorder tile appears. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin it so it's always visible.

How to record

  1. Click the AFS Screen Recorder icon in the Chrome toolbar.
  2. In the popup, toggle Camera bubble and/or Microphone on or off as needed. Your choices are remembered.
  3. Click Start Recording.
  4. Chrome will ask what to capture. Choose a screen, window, or tab, then click Share.
  5. Recording begins. A webcam bubble appears on the page - drag it to any corner to reposition. The recording toolbar gives you Pause / Resume and Stop buttons. Both follow you as you switch tabs.
  6. To finish, click Stop in the toolbar, or click Chrome's native Stop sharing bar at the bottom of the screen - either will end the recording cleanly.
  7. A result page opens automatically. Click Download to save the .webm file, or click Open My Drive to go to Google Drive and drag the file in for a shareable link.
Switching tabs mid-recording. The bubble and toolbar reappear on each new tab you open. Your recording continues uninterrupted in the background.

Troubleshooting

The bubble and toolbar do not appear on some pages

Chrome does not allow extensions to inject overlays onto chrome:// pages, the Chrome Web Store, or the built-in PDF viewer. Recording still works normally and your camera is still composited into the video. The on-page controls simply cannot show on those restricted pages.

My webcam is not in the recording

When you first click Start Recording, Chrome will prompt for camera permission. Click Allow. If you previously dismissed or blocked the prompt, click the lock or tune icon in the address bar, find Camera, set it to Allow, and reload the page before trying again.

The video will not play in some apps

The recording is saved as a .webm file. It plays natively in Chrome and in Google Drive's built-in player. If another app cannot open it, upload it to Google Drive and play it there, or share the Drive link directly.

Part 2

How it works

Chrome Extension (MV3) MediaRecorder desktopCapture Canvas Compositing

1 Architecture

Service Worker
orchestrates state, routes messages
→drives
Offscreen Document
camera + screen via desktopCapture, composited on canvas, recorded with MediaRecorder
→paints
Content Script
bubble + toolbar overlay on each tab

Manifest V3. The hidden offscreen document runs the recording engine so capture survives tab switches without interruption. The content script paints the draggable bubble and toolbar onto whatever page you are viewing.

FileRole
manifest.jsonPermissions, service worker registration, content script injection
src/background.jsService worker - state machine, offscreen document lifecycle, message routing
src/offscreen.jsRecording engine - camera capture, screen capture, canvas compositor, MediaRecorder
src/content.jsInjects the draggable bubble and toolbar; relays pause/stop commands to the service worker
src/popup.html/jsThe toolbar popup for toggling camera and mic, and starting a recording
src/result.html/jsPost-recording page: inline preview, download button, Drive link
src/lib/Shared utilities: canvas compositing, MIME detection, filename generation, shared state

2 Footprint and cost

Zero. The extension is fully local. There are no servers, no accounts, no API keys, and nothing to maintain or pay for. The only network activity is the Google Drive tab that opens when you choose to share a recording - that is your own Drive, not anything run by the extension.

No ongoing cost. Unlike Loom or similar tools, there is no monthly fee, no storage limit, and no third-party data handling.

3 Privacy and security

  • Recordings never leave your machine unless you explicitly upload them.
  • The extension stores nothing remotely and has no backend of any kind.
  • Camera and microphone data is captured and composited entirely in the browser, in the offscreen document, without any external calls.
  • Sharing is a deliberate manual step - drag the file into Drive yourself.

4 Limitations

  • The floating bubble overlays web pages only, not native desktop apps. The camera is still baked into the recording everywhere - you just will not see the bubble while you are in a native app.
  • Output format is .webm. It plays in Chrome and Google Drive. For other apps, upload to Drive and use the Drive player or shareable link.
  • Sharing requires a manual drag into Google Drive. There is no one-click "copy link" built in.
  • Internal AFS use only. Not published to the Chrome Web Store.
